DMCA Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-02 · Ravomix

Ravomix respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects its users and contributors to do the same. We respond to clear and complete notices of alleged copyright infringement that comply with the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and the relevant provisions of the Indian Copyright Act, 1957 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. This page explains how to submit a takedown request and how we handle it.

1. Our Copyright Policy

If you believe that any content available on Ravomix infringes a copyright that you own or are authorised to enforce, you may submit a written notice to our designated agent at the email address below. We review every notice on its merits, and we may remove or disable access to the allegedly infringing material while we investigate, in line with industry best practice and applicable law.

2. How to Submit a DMCA Takedown Notice

To be effective, your notice must be in writing and include all of the following information:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorised to act on their behalf;
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (or, if multiple works are covered by a single notice, a representative list of the works);
  3. The exact URL(s) on Ravomix where the allegedly infringing material appears, with enough detail for us to locate it;
  4. Your full legal name, postal address, telephone number, and email address;
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law; and
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or are authorised to act on the owner’s behalf.

3. Where to Send Your Notice

Send your complete notice to ravomixsupport@gmail.com with the subject line “DMCA Takedown — [name of work]”. We strongly recommend sending notices by email; postal notices may take significantly longer to process.

4. Counter-Notice Procedure

If you believe that material you posted (or that links to your work) was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notice. The counter-notice must include your full contact details, identification of the material that was removed, a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief that the removal was an error, and your consent to the jurisdiction of the appropriate Indian courts (or, if outside India, the courts of your domicile and the Republic of India).

5. Repeat Infringers

It is Ravomix’s policy, in appropriate circumstances and at our sole discretion, to disable or terminate access for users or contributors who are deemed to be repeat infringers.

6. False Claims

Please be aware that under Section 512(f) of the DMCA and the corresponding Indian provisions, any person who knowingly makes a material misrepresentation in a takedown notice or counter-notice may be liable for damages, including costs and lawyers’ fees. Submit a notice only if you genuinely believe your rights are being infringed.

7. Contact

All copyright queries: ravomixsupport@gmail.com.
