About Ravomix

Last updated: 2026-05-02 · By Ravomix Team

Ravomix is a free, ad-supported Indian utility platform built to make everyday paperwork, financial calculations, and digital tools effortless for ordinary citizens, students, freelancers, and small business owners. We bring together more than 200 ready-to-use application and complaint letter templates and over 50 everyday calculators and converters under one fast, mobile-friendly roof. Whether you need a leave application for school, a transfer request for your office, a consumer complaint draft for an e-commerce dispute, or a quick EMI, GST, SIP, or income-tax calculation, Ravomix gives you a polished result in seconds — in both Hindi and English.

What We Offer

Our content library is organised into three core verticals so users can find what they need without confusion:

  • Applications: 100+ professionally drafted formats including school leave applications, job applications, transfer requests, scholarship requests, RTI applications, character certificate requests, NOC letters, and more.
  • Complaints: 100+ legally-aware complaint formats covering consumer grievances, banking ombudsman, police complaints, RTO complaints, telecom regulator (TRAI), workplace harassment, electricity board, municipal corporation, and many other Indian authorities.
  • Tools & Calculators: 50+ utilities — EMI calculator, GST calculator, income-tax calculator, SIP / FD / RD / PPF calculators, BMI, age calculator, percentage, unit converters, password generator, character counter, time-zone converter, currency converter and more.

Who Ravomix Is For

Ravomix is built for the Indian user. Students preparing leave letters or scholarship applications, employees writing transfer or resignation requests, parents submitting school correspondence, consumers fighting unfair charges from banks or e-commerce platforms, freelancers calculating GST and tax outgo, and senior citizens who simply want a clean, large-text format they can fill in and print — every one of these users is at the centre of how we design our pages. Every template is editable, downloadable, printable, and can be saved as a PDF directly from the browser.

Why We Built Ravomix

Most Indians spend hours searching the internet for the “correct” format of a simple letter, only to land on cluttered websites filled with intrusive pop-ups, paywalls, or out-of-date drafts. Calculators are scattered across dozens of single-purpose sites, each with its own ad-heavy interface. Ravomix exists to solve this fragmentation: one trusted destination, free forever for the end user, with bilingual content (Hindi + English) and zero forced sign-ups.

Our Editorial Standards

Every template on Ravomix is reviewed by writers familiar with Indian administrative practice, current as of 2025. Calculators use the latest published rates (income-tax slabs for FY 2024-25, GST slabs as notified by CBIC, RBI repo-linked rates where applicable). We mark advisory content clearly and recommend professional consultation for legal, medical, or large financial decisions.

Privacy at a Glance

Ravomix does not require an account. Your drafts are stored only in your own browser using local storage; we never upload them to a server. We use Google Analytics to understand which tools and templates are popular, and Google AdSense to display ads that keep the service free. You can read the full Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, and Terms of Service for details.
