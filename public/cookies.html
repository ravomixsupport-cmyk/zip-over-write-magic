Cookie Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-02 · Ravomix

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies Ravomix uses, why we use them, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit it. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide reporting information to the site owners. Cookies set by the website you are visiting are called “first-party cookies”. Cookies set by other parties (for example, an advertising or analytics provider) are called “third-party cookies”.

2. Cookies Used on Ravomix

Ravomix uses the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Required for the basic operation of the site, such as remembering whether you have accepted our welcome disclaimer or chosen Hindi vs English. These cookies cannot be switched off.
  • Preference cookies: Store non-essential preferences such as your selected currency in calculators or your default tool category.
  • Analytics cookies: Set by Google Analytics (e.g. _ga, _gid) to help us understand how visitors interact with the site, in aggregated and anonymised form.
  • Advertising cookies: Set by Google AdSense (e.g. __gads, IDE, NID) and its partners to deliver ads that are more relevant to you, to limit the number of times you see the same ad, and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect information about how visitors use Ravomix. The cookies collect information in an aggregated form, including the number of visitors, where visitors have come from, and the pages they visited. To opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

4. Google AdSense

Ravomix is supported by Google AdSense, which uses cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to our website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you. You can disable personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or you can opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

5. How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies, delete cookies already on your device, or alert you when a cookie is being set. Below are links to the help pages of common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of Ravomix and many other websites you visit.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The “Effective date” at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
