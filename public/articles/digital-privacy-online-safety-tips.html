Cybercrime Complaint Letter & Online Fraud Reporting Format (India)

By Ravomix Editorial · Updated 2025-04-15 · Category: Complaints

If you have lost money to a UPI fraud, received threats on social media, or had your account hacked, you can file a formal cybercrime complaint in India. This guide explains the exact letter format accepted by the Cyber Crime Cell and the steps to report on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCRP, cybercrime.gov.in).

Where to report a cybercrime in India

  1. NCRP portal — cybercrime.gov.in (24x7, all states).
  2. Helpline 1930 — for financial frauds (UPI, bank, wallet) within the "golden hour".
  3. Local Cyber Crime Police Station — written complaint addressed to the Inspector / SHO.
  4. Banking Ombudsman — for unresolved bank-side fraud after 30 days.

Cybercrime complaint letter format

To,
The Inspector,
Cyber Crime Police Station,
[District / City Name]

Subject: Complaint regarding online financial fraud / hacking of [account name] on [date]

Respected Sir/Madam,

I, [Full Name], aged [__], resident of [full address], holder of mobile number [__] and PAN [__], wish to file a formal complaint regarding a cybercrime committed against me on [date] at approximately [time].

Brief facts of the case:
1. On [date], I received a [call / SMS / WhatsApp message / email] from [number / email ID] claiming to be from [bank / company].
2. The accused convinced me to [share OTP / click a link / install an app / make a UPI payment].
3. As a result, an amount of ₹[amount] was debited from my [bank name] account number XXXX[last 4] via [UPI ID / transaction ID].
4. The transaction reference number is [__] dated [__].

I request you to kindly register an FIR under the relevant sections of the IT Act, 2000 and IPC, freeze the beneficiary account, and take necessary action to recover the amount.

The following documents are enclosed:
(i) Bank statement showing the disputed transaction
(ii) Screenshots of the messages / call logs
(iii) Copy of Aadhaar / PAN as ID proof
(iv) NCRP acknowledgement number, if already filed

Yours faithfully,
[Signature]
[Full Name]   [Mobile]   [Email]
Date: [__]   Place: [__]

Step-by-step: Filing on the NCRP portal

  1. Visit cybercrime.gov.in and click "File a Complaint".
  2. Select category — Financial Fraud, Women/Child related, or Other Cybercrime.
  3. Enter your mobile number and verify with OTP.
  4. Fill victim details, accused details (if known) and incident details.
  5. Upload supporting documents — screenshots, bank statements, ID proof.
  6. Submit and save the Acknowledgement Number. You can track status with this number.

What to do in the first hour of a UPI fraud

  • Call 1930 immediately — the sooner you report, the higher the chance of freezing the beneficiary account.
  • Inform your bank and request transaction reversal in writing (use a Bank Complaint template).
  • Disable UPI in your apps and change your UPI PIN.
  • File a complaint on NCRP within 24 hours.
