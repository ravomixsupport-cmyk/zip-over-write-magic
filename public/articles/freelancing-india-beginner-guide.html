Freelance Invoice, GST Bill & Client Agreement Letter Formats (India)

By Ravomix Editorial · Updated 2025-04-15 · Category: Templates

Indian freelancers — designers, writers, developers, consultants — need three documents on repeat: a clean invoice, a valid GST bill (if registered), and a clear client agreement letter. This guide gives you all three formats plus a polite payment reminder letter you can copy directly.

1. Freelance Invoice Format (Non-GST)

If your annual turnover is below the GST threshold (₹20 lakh for services in most states), you can issue a simple invoice without GST.

Mandatory fields

  • Invoice number (sequential, e.g. INV-2025-014) and date.
  • Your name / firm name, address, PAN, mobile, email.
  • Client's name, address, GSTIN (if any).
  • Description of services, period, quantity / hours, rate, amount.
  • Total payable in figures and words.
  • Bank details — Account name, A/c number, IFSC, UPI ID.
  • Signature of the freelancer.

2. GST Tax Invoice Format (For Registered Freelancers)

Once registered, every invoice must follow Rule 46 of the CGST Rules.

Required fields under GST

  1. Supplier's name, address and GSTIN.
  2. Unique consecutive invoice number (max 16 characters).
  3. Date of issue.
  4. Recipient's name, address, GSTIN/UIN (if registered).
  5. HSN/SAC code — for most freelance services use SAC 9983 / 9984 / 9987 as applicable.
  6. Description, value, taxable value.
  7. CGST + SGST (intra-state) or IGST (inter-state / export of services).
  8. Place of supply.
  9. Signature or digital signature.

3. Freelance Client Agreement Letter Format

Always confirm a project in writing — even a one-page agreement letter prevents 90% of payment disputes.

Freelance Service Agreement Letter
Date: [__]

Between: [Freelancer name], [address] — "the Service Provider"
And: [Client name / company], [address] — "the Client"

1. Scope of Work: The Service Provider will deliver [describe deliverables] as per the brief dated [__].
2. Timeline: The work will commence on [__] and be delivered on or before [__].
3. Fees: Total professional fees ₹[__] (plus GST if applicable). 50% advance on signing, 50% on delivery.
4. Revisions: Up to [2] rounds of revisions are included. Additional revisions billed at ₹[__] per hour.
5. Ownership: Final deliverables transfer to the Client only after full payment is received.
6. Confidentiality: Both parties agree not to disclose any confidential information shared during the engagement.
7. Termination: Either party may terminate with 7 days' written notice. Work completed till date will be billed.
8. Jurisdiction: Disputes will be subject to the courts of [city].

Signed:
Service Provider: ____________    Client: ____________

4. Payment Reminder Letter (Polite, Firm, Final)

Polite reminder (3-7 days overdue)

Subject: Friendly reminder — Invoice INV-2025-014 due

Dear [Client], hope you are well. Just a gentle reminder that invoice INV-2025-014 dated [__] for ₹[__] is now due. Kindly arrange the payment at your earliest convenience. Bank details are on the invoice. Thank you!

Firm reminder (15+ days overdue)

Subject: Second reminder — Invoice INV-2025-014 (overdue by 15 days)

Dear [Client], this is a follow-up to our earlier reminder. As per our agreement dated [__], the payment of ₹[__] was due on [__] and is now overdue by 15 days. Kindly clear the dues by [__] to avoid late-payment charges as per our agreement.

Final notice (before legal notice)

Subject: Final notice — Invoice INV-2025-014

Dear [Client], despite our reminders, the dues of ₹[__] remain unpaid. This is a final notice. Please clear the amount within 7 days, failing which we will be constrained to issue a formal legal notice and initiate recovery proceedings.
