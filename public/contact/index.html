Contact Ravomix

We usually respond within 24–48 hours on working days.

We love hearing from our users. Whether you’ve found a typo in a template, want to request a new calculator, need help downloading a PDF, or wish to discuss a partnership or copyright matter, the Ravomix team is reachable through the channels listed below. Please choose the option that best fits your query so we can route it to the right person and reply faster.

Email

The fastest and most reliable way to reach us is by email. Write to ravomixsupport@gmail.com with a clear subject line. For example: “Bug in EMI calculator”, “New template request — Maternity leave application”, or “DMCA takedown request”. Including a screenshot, the URL of the affected page, and the device/browser you’re using helps us reproduce and fix issues much faster.

Social Media

  • Twitter / X: @ravomix — product updates, quick tips, and announcements.
  • Instagram: @ravomixai — visual guides, design previews, and behind-the-scenes content.

Please note that direct messages on social media are checked less frequently than email. For anything time-sensitive (lost data, billing-related questions, copyright complaints) please always email ravomixsupport@gmail.com.

Response Time

Our small team aims to acknowledge every email within 24 hours on Indian working days (Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 7 PM IST), and to provide a substantive answer within 2–3 working days. Complex requests such as DMCA takedowns or new feature evaluations may take up to seven working days. If you have not received a reply within five working days, please check your spam folder and then send a polite follow-up.

Press & Partnerships

Journalists, educators, and organisations interested in collaborating with Ravomix on content, surveys, or co-branded calculators can write to the same email address with “Partnership” in the subject line. We are particularly interested in collaborations that improve digital literacy, financial awareness, and citizen rights in India.
