What Ravomix Offers

Ravomix focuses on common Indian paperwork and daily digital needs. Many people search the internet for a leave application, job application, transfer request, rent agreement note, police complaint, bank complaint, consumer complaint, scholarship request, RTI application, or legal notice format. They often find outdated examples, confusing wording, or pages that are difficult to use on mobile. Ravomix solves this by offering structured formats that can be opened quickly, understood easily, and adapted for real situations.

The Applications section includes formats for students, employees, parents, tenants, customers, account holders, and citizens. Users can find school leave applications, office leave applications, job application letters, resignation letters, transfer applications, permission letters, character certificate requests, NOC requests, fee concession applications, scholarship applications, and other everyday formats. These templates are written in a simple, respectful tone so they can be edited for different recipients.

The Complaints section is built for users who need to explain a problem clearly. It includes formats for consumer complaints, banking issues, e-commerce disputes, defective products, delayed services, police complaints, cyber complaints, electricity board complaints, municipal complaints, telecom complaints, workplace harassment complaints, landlord-tenant concerns, and other grievance situations. Ravomix does not replace professional legal advice, but it helps users prepare a clear first draft with the correct structure: sender details, recipient details, subject line, facts, request for action, and supporting documents.

The Tools section provides everyday calculators and utilities. Users can calculate EMI, GST, SIP, FD, RD, PPF, simple interest, compound interest, salary, income tax, profit and loss, discount, age, BMI, mileage, fuel cost, electricity bill, percentage, marks percentage, rent split, and tips. It also includes converters for length, weight, temperature, area, speed, time, CSS units, number bases, Roman numerals, and currencies. Text tools such as word counter, character counter, text case converter, lorem ipsum generator, hash generator, password generator, random number generator, colour picker, stopwatch, world clock, and time-zone tools are included for students, creators, developers, and office users.

The Articles section explains useful topics in plain language. It covers application writing tips, complaint filing steps, consumer rights, banking ombudsman guidance, GST calculator usage, EMI calculator basics, tax-saving ideas, resume writing, interview preparation, freelancing in India, online business registration, digital privacy, strong passwords, QR code uses, PDF converter guidance, image resizing, rent agreement rules, power of attorney basics, will formats, legal notice formats, RTI applications, and workplace complaint guidance. These articles help users understand not only what to write, but why a format or tool works.
