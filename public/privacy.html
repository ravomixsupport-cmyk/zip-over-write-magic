Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-02 · Ravomix

This Privacy Policy explains how Ravomix (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit our website and use our templates, calculators, and tools. By using Ravomix you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

1. Information We Collect

Ravomix is designed to require the minimum amount of personal data possible. We do not require you to create an account. The information we may collect falls into the following categories:

  • Information you provide voluntarily: Text you type into a template or calculator. This data stays in your browser’s local storage and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Information collected automatically: Standard log data such as IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and approximate location (city / country) derived from your IP. This data is collected via Google Analytics.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: Small text files stored on your device. See our separate Cookie Policy for details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Ravomix website and its features;
  • Understand which templates and tools are most useful so we can prioritise updates;
  • Detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, security incidents, and abuse;
  • Display relevant advertising through Google AdSense; and
  • Comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

3. Cookies

Ravomix uses both first-party and third-party cookies. First-party cookies remember your language preference and acceptance of our welcome disclaimer. Third-party cookies are set by Google Analytics and Google AdSense (see sections 4 and 5). You may delete or block cookies using your browser settings, but parts of the site may stop functioning correctly.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregated, anonymised usage patterns. Google Analytics may set cookies such as _ga and _gid. The information generated is transmitted to and stored by Google. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on.

5. Google AdSense

Ravomix is supported by advertising delivered through Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to Ravomix and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites such as government portals, banks, or reference resources. We do not control these websites and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before submitting any information.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures (HTTPS encryption, restricted hosting access, periodic dependency updates) to protect against unauthorised access. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

Ravomix is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 of India), you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal information we hold about you. Because Ravomix does not require accounts and does not store the content you type, most “personal data” requests will only relate to analytics or advertising identifiers, which you can manage through Google’s tools linked above.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of Ravomix after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact for Privacy Queries

For any questions, complaints, or requests regarding this policy or your data, please email us at ravomixsupport@gmail.com. We aim to respond within five working days.
